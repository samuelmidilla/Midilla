What this output is

Legal content that does not compromise accuracy for readability.

Legal content written without a documented accuracy system produces one of two failures: content that is accurate but unreadable, or content that is readable but legally imprecise. The Legal Content Bible was built to eliminate both failure modes.

Every output passes through 17 quality gates specifically designed for legal content — accuracy checks that no general content system applies. The result is content that meets editorial standards and legal precision standards simultaneously.

The Standard

"Accuracy is not a preference in legal content. It is the gate. Every claim, every statistic, every jurisdictional reference passes verification before the output is delivered."

What is delivered

Four output types. One accuracy standard.

01
Practice Area Pages

Structured content pages for specific legal practice areas — accurate, readable, and optimised for the queries potential clients are actually searching.

02
Attorney Profiles

Biographical profiles that communicate expertise without overclaiming. Every credential referenced is accurate. The voice is authoritative and human simultaneously.

03
Legal Thought Leadership

Articles and commentary for attorneys building public authority — structured arguments on legal developments, precedents, and practice area insights.

04
Legal Marketing Copy

Website copy, service descriptions, and positioning statements for law firms. Claims are defensible. No generic volume language. No unverified superlatives.

The 17-gate accuracy system

More quality gates than any general content system applies.

The Legal Content Bible contains 17 quality gates that apply specifically to legal content — gates that verify accuracy, jurisdictional correctness, claim defensibility, and ethical compliance before delivery. The 4-gate standard that governs all MIDILLA outputs is applied in addition to these 17 legal-specific gates.

// Selected Gates — Legal Content Bible
L-01
Jurisdictional accuracy — all jurisdictional references are correct for the stated region and practice context.
L-04
Claim defensibility — every stated claim can be defended against scrutiny. No overreach. No unverified assertions.
L-09
Credential verification — all referenced credentials, bar admissions, and qualifications match what was supplied in the brief.
L-14
Ethical compliance — content complies with legal marketing ethics guidelines applicable to the stated jurisdiction.
L-17
Final accuracy pass — independent review of all factual claims against supplied evidence before delivery.
