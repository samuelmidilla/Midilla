Common Questions

Direct answers.
No filler.

What is a credit and how many do I need?
A credit is the unit of production on this platform. A 3,000-word white paper costs 12 credits. A 5-email sequence costs 8 credits. The Starter tier (60 credits/month) supports consistent single-document production. The Professional tier (250 credits/month) supports sustained multi-output production.
How is MIDILLA different from an AI writing tool?
AI writing tools give you access to an engine. You provide the production methodology. MIDILLA provides the methodology — built and documented before your brief arrived. The quality of your output is not determined by how well you write the brief. It is determined by the architecture of the production system applied to it.
What if the output doesn't meet my standard?
Every output passes a documented quality gate before delivery. The gate evaluates structural completeness, voice consistency, argument coherence, and source quality. If a delivered output contains a documented structural error, it is corrected under the same quality standard as the original production. This is not a satisfaction guarantee. It is a production standard with a documented revision protocol.
How is the delivery time calculated?
The production time for every output type has been tested and documented. The delivery time is stated on your confirmation screen as an exact clock time ("Delivers at 14:30 WAT"), not a duration. The E-03 delivery email arrives at the confirmed time. Not approximately. Exactly.
Is there a setup process before my first order?
No. Account creation, tier selection, and first order can be completed in a single session. The system does not require onboarding, a consultation, or a discovery call before any output is produced.
Who is this platform for?
Professional service operators — consultants, lawyers, financial advisors, agency owners — who need publication-ready written content at expert level and do not have the production infrastructure to create it consistently. This is not a general-purpose writing tool.
