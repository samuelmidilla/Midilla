Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2026 · MIDILLA · Samuel Ibrahim
Overview

MIDILLA is operated by Samuel Ibrahim. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information provided when you interact with MIDILLA — including through our website, brief intake form, and any communication arising from a service engagement.

This policy is written in plain language. It states clearly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Nothing in this policy is designed to obscure our practices.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you submit a brief through our intake form. This includes:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your organisation or practice name, if provided
  • The content of your brief — the description of the output you are requesting
  • Your selected output type and production tier
  • Your stated target audience
  • Any files you attach to your brief submission

We do not collect payment information through this website. Payment processing, when applicable, is handled through a third-party processor and is governed by that processor's privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through the brief intake form is used exclusively to:

  • Process and respond to your brief submission
  • Produce the content output you have requested
  • Communicate with you about your order — including confirmation, production status, and delivery
  • Improve the intake system and production process based on the types of briefs received

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising purposes. We do not share your brief contents with any party not involved in production of your order.

Form Submission Processing

Brief submissions on this website are processed through Formspree, a third-party form processing service. When you submit the brief intake form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to our designated inbox. Formspree's handling of your data is governed by their privacy policy, available at formspree.io.

Data Retention

We retain brief submission data for the duration required to complete your order and for a reasonable period thereafter to support any follow-up questions or disputes. We do not retain your information indefinitely. To request deletion of your data, contact us through the brief intake form on the homepage.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to request deletion of your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the brief intake form on the homepage.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the platform after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
