The production window stated for each output type begins at the point of order confirmation, not at the point of brief submission.

MIDILLA produces content based on the information provided in the client's brief. The accuracy of the output depends on the accuracy of the brief. The client is responsible for:

MIDILLA applies quality gates to every output but does not independently verify third-party data or claims provided by the client in the brief.

Revisions

MIDILLA's quality standard is designed to produce publication-ready output without revision. If a delivered output does not meet the specifications stated in the original brief, MIDILLA will revise the output. Revision requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery and must reference specific elements of the brief that the output does not satisfy.

Revision requests that introduce new requirements not present in the original brief are treated as new orders.
