The Service

MIDILLA is a content production platform. It produces written outputs — including but not limited to B2B white papers, legal content, email sequences, LinkedIn content calendars, personal finance articles, and ecommerce SEO content — in exchange for credit-based subscription payments.

Every output is produced through a documented production system. The quality standard applied is the publication-ready standard — meaning the delivered document does not require editorial revision before it reaches its intended audience.

Briefs and Orders

An order begins when a brief is submitted through the intake form and confirmed by MIDILLA. Confirmation is sent to the email address provided in the brief. The production window begins on confirmation, not on submission.

The production window stated for each output type is a maximum. MIDILLA consistently delivers before the window closes. If a delivery is delayed beyond the stated window, you will be notified directly.

The quality of the output is governed by the production Bible applied to the brief. The more specific and complete the brief, the more precisely the system can be applied.

Revisions

MIDILLA's quality standard is designed to produce publication-ready output without revision. If a delivered output does not satisfy requirements stated in the original brief, it will be revised at no additional credit cost.

Revision requests must:

  • Be submitted within 7 days of delivery
  • Reference specific requirements stated in the original brief that the output does not satisfy
  • Not introduce new requirements not present in the original brief

Revision requests that introduce new requirements are treated as new briefs and consume credits accordingly.

Credits and Billing

Credits are the unit of production on MIDILLA. Each output type has a documented credit cost. Subscription tiers determine credit allocation per billing cycle. Unused credits do not roll over to the next cycle.

Subscription payments are processed through a third-party payment processor. MIDILLA does not store payment information. Billing disputes are handled through the payment processor's dispute mechanism.
